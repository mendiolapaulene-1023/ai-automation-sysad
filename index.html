Workflow Automation

01. Customer Support Ticket Management System

Built with Zoho Creator to organise incoming customer inquiries, route requests to the appropriate team, and assign priority levels based on predefined rules.

Key Features:

  • Ticket number generation
  • Customer full name and email tracking
  • Inquiry type categorisation
  • Team or department assignment
  • Low, Medium, and High priority classification
  • Ticket status tracking
  • Date received and follow-up date tracking

Workflow: Customer inquiry → Ticket creation → Classification → Team assignment → Priority setting → Status and follow-up tracking.

Platform: Zoho Creator
